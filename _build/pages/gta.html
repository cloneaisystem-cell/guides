4 steps

  1. Use game renders as references

    Upload a clean render of each character and tell the model which reference is who, in the frame's own left and right.

  2. Make the start frame

    One still per scene with GPT Image 2.5 on Dreamina (11 credits per image). This is the Michael frame from reel 3: 
    REFERENCE ROLES: reference 1 = Michael, exact same face, slicked-back dark hair, grey suit jacket, open white shirt, build and age. Do not change his face.
Photorealistic cinematic film still, 50mm lens, 4:3. Michael's Rockford Hills mansion at golden-hour sunset. Michael sits on the edge of a white sun lounger beside his turquoise swimming pool, leaning forward, elbows on his knees, a crystal whisky glass with amber whisky held loosely in his right hand at chest height. He looks straight into the camera with a tired, fond half-smile, lips closed, like an old friend saying goodbye. Camera at his seated eye level, about two metres away, medium shot from the knees up, Michael slightly right of centre. Behind him, out of focus: the pool with sparkling reflections, white stone arches, palm trees and the Los Santos skyline. Low warm sun from the right, soft lens flare at the frame edge. Natural skin texture, no text, no logos, no watermark.

  3. Ask for three shots in one clip

    Seedance 2.5 on Dreamina, "any reference" mode: 7 seconds, 3 shots, 259 credits (37 per second at 720p). This is the pier clip from reel 3: 
    Video style: photorealistic cinematic live-action film, 35mm film look, the last minutes of golden-hour sunset on the wooden Del Perro pier, warm nostalgic haze, natural realistic motion, 24 fps, soft anamorphic flares. Three shots joined by hard cuts, all on the same pier in the same light. Every character looks exactly like his reference: same face, hair, clothes and build.
REFERENCES: reference 1 = Franklin (left, dark navy shirt over white t-shirt), Michael (right, grey suit) and the Rottweiler Chop (green collar) on the pier; shot 1 starts exactly like this image. reference 2 = Trevor (balding, messy hair at the sides, dirty white t-shirt, jeans).

  4. Cut on the beat

    Put the clips on the song and cut only on its beat grid. The last word gets a black frame. 
    Beat grid: 0.3745 s (measured on See You Again, librosa).
Land every cut on a multiple of the grid, within 1 frame.
Last lyric "again" = black frame, cut on the beat at 84.92 s of the track.
Loudness -14 LUFS, the same level Instagram plays reels at.

Mistakes I hit

  • Big faces of the GTA 6 heroes got my clip blocked twice. Keep them small in frame and it passes.
  • I edited a 2K frame where the face was 200 px wide. The model kept the old face. Crop in first.
  • A free face-swap gave a soft grey face. Regenerating with good references looked far better.
