The 13 skills

  • /ig-reel turns one idea into a reel: 3 hooks from 26 formulas, scored, then the script, on-screen text and a timed beat sheet.
  • /ig-viral finds what's working in your niche, ranks reels by how far they beat their own account's median and names the formula.
  • /ig-caption writes the caption and shows exactly what appears before "... more".
  • /ig-carousel makes the cover, the slide copy and the 1080×1350 files.
  • /ig-story plans the daily story sequence and the DM funnel behind it.
  • /ig-profile scores your profile against a 12-part rubric and rewrites the weak parts first.
  • /ig-plan plans your week: what, which format, when, and 10 accounts to engage with.
  • /ig-human strips AI tells (em dashes, slop words) from any draft.
  • /ig-comment writes comments for other people's posts, picked by what the post is.
  • /ig-reply sorts the comments under your post and answers them in order.
  • /ig-dm writes the keyword delivery, the first message, a collab pitch and two follow-ups.
  • /ig-repurpose turns one video or newsletter into a week of posts.
  • /ig-audit is a post-mortem on what you already posted.

Your first 15 minutes

  1. /ig-viral + your niche: the swipe file of what's working this week.
  2. /ig-reel on the best idea: 3 scored hooks and the beat sheet. Film it.
  3. /ig-profile: fix the top 3 things it flags.
These skills write, they don't post. You stay in control of your account.
