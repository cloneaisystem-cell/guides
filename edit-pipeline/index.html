The pipeline in 4 steps

  1. Setup (once)

    1. Download the Claude desktop app (claude.ai/download) and open the Code tab.
    2. Make a folder called AI Video Editor with three folders inside: raw-footage, references, exports.
    3. New chat in Code, choose Local, then select the AI Video Editor folder.

  2. Install the 4 free tools

    HyperFrames (motion graphics from HTML), FFmpeg (cuts, audio, export), Whisper (word-level captions) and yt-dlp (downloads reference reels so Claude can study them). Paste this: 
    You're my video editor. We work in this folder: raw-footage, references, exports.
Install and check these 4 free tools, official installs only:
1. FFmpeg (winget on Windows, Homebrew on Mac)
2. faster-whisper in a .venv in this folder, for word-level captions
3. yt-dlp, to download reference reels
4. HyperFrames (npx hyperframes), for motion graphics; needs Node.js 22+
Check what's already installed first. Then test each one for real: cut a 3-second test clip, transcribe 5 seconds of audio, render a 2-second HyperFrames title. Show me a table: tool, version, works yes/no.

  3. Teach it your style

    Pick 3 to 5 reels from a creator whose editing you love and paste their links: 
    Here are reels whose editing I like: [link 1] [link 2] [link 3].
Download them with yt-dlp into references (for study only).
Study each one frame by frame and by ear: hook in the first 3 seconds, shot length, cut rhythm, captions (font, size, colour, position, words per line), colours, graphics, transitions, sound effects and music level.
Write my style to style.md with exact values (seconds, pixels, hex colours, dB). Take inspiration, don't copy their shots, text or logos.
Show me style.md and ask what to change.

  4. Edit your video

    Drop your recording into raw-footage, then paste: 
    My new recording is in raw-footage. Edit it with style.md:
1. Transcribe it with Whisper, word timestamps. Ask me about any word you're unsure of.
2. Show me a storyboard first: one line per scene with the time, what's on screen and the caption. Wait for my OK.
3. Then cut the pauses and bad takes, keep the last good take, add word-by-word captions in my style, graphics only where they help, sound effects on the cuts.
4. Export 1080x1920 to exports as draft-v1.mp4 and write edit-notes.md.
Check the file yourself before you show me.

    Ask for changes in plain words, like "make the font bigger" or "cut faster in the first 5 seconds". Claude saves draft-v2, draft-v3 and so on.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Don't skip the storyboard. Fixing a plan is faster than fixing a render.
  • Keep reference reels for study only. Take the style, never their shots, text or logos.
  • One change request at a time. Then you can tell which one made it better.
