My story

Three years of building with AI, every day.

Based inRomania
LanguagesRomanian, English
Time zoneRomania (EET), overlaps Europe and the US morning
Focus since2023
Best fitSmall businesses, creators, startups

Three years ago, when ChatGPT arrived, I made a decision: I would find out what AI can really build for people who run real businesses.

Three years later it is still all I do. I began with text and code, then added design, 3D, video, ads and automation as each one became good enough for client work. I have built websites for local businesses, apps, ad creatives, and automated email and calendar workflows.

Today I use AI to write and design, to code and test, to shoot and edit video, to make ads and to automate the boring parts. Where one tool ends, I connect the next one. You get one finished result from one person.

I also run an online community where I teach other people to use these tools. Teaching changed how I work. When you explain every step to someone else, your process stops depending on luck. It becomes clear, repeatable and written down.

As a client, you get a plan you can read, work you can see early, and files and notes you keep. I am not a big agency, and that is the point. You work with me directly.

Good to know

Questions clients ask before hiring.

I am not technical. Can we still work together?

Yes. Most of my clients are not technical. I explain things in plain words and take care of hosting, setup and the details.

Do you use AI for everything?

I use AI to work faster and try more ideas, and I check every result myself. You get work that is reviewed by a person who is responsible for it.

Can I see how you work before I hire you?

Yes. Open the walkthroughs above, or send me a short brief and I will tell you how I would approach it.

What do I receive at the end?

The finished project, the source files, and a short written guide on how it works and how to change it.

How does pricing work?

After a short chat about your goal, I send a fixed price and a plan. You know the cost before I start, so there are no hourly surprises.

How long does a project take?

Small tasks take days. Bigger projects are split into milestones, and I give you a date for each one before we begin.

Can you keep my project private?

Yes. I sign an NDA on request and I do not share your files, photos or business details.
